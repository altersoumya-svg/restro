Who we are

Twenty-four years of cooking for the same neighbourhood

Caferio is a family restaurant that happens to keep winning awards. Two generations, one oak-fired oven, and a stubborn belief that hospitality is a craft rather than a script.

We seat 84 guests across the main room, the alcove and the terrace. Everything on the plate — bread, pasta, stock, ice cream, pastry — is made in this building.

Reserve a Table
Our mission

Cook honestly, source locally, serve warmly

Every dish must justify itself on three counts: where the ingredient came from, what the fire did to it, and whether it makes you want a second bite. If a plate fails one, it leaves the menu.

Our vision

A room worth crossing the city for

To be the restaurant people book for the moments that matter — and the café they still drop into on an ordinary Tuesday morning. Luxury without distance.

Milestones

How the room took shape

  • 2002

    Eight tables on Lantern Row

    Maria and Elias Caferio open a small café with one espresso machine and a borrowed oven.

  • 2008

    The wood oven arrives

    A three-tonne oak-fired oven is craned through the back wall and never switched off since.

  • 2014

    Marco takes the pass

    Executive Chef Marco Alvarez joins from Modena and writes the first fully seasonal carte.

  • 2019

    The dry-aging room

    A glass-fronted cabinet lets guests watch beef mature for forty days beside the bar.

  • 2023

    Our own roastery

    Three streets away, we begin roasting every single-origin lot we serve.

  • 2026

    Chef's Table Week

    Twelve seats a night at the pass, and the busiest year in our history.
