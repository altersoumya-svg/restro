Our story

A neighbourhood room that grew into a destination

Caferio began in 2002 as an eight-table café on Lantern Row with one espresso machine and a borrowed oven. Twenty-four years later the oven burns oak, the coffee is roasted three streets away, and the room still fills with the same regulars.

Everything is made in-house: bread at 5am, stocks overnight, pastry before service. What we cannot make well, we buy from people who can.

  • Produce from farms within forty miles
  • Wood-fired oven and 40-day dry-aging cabinet
  • Single-origin coffee, roasted weekly
