Truffle Wild Mushroom Risotto

★★★★★ 4.9 · 212 reviews

$24.50

Carnaroli rice cooked in a mushroom and thyme stock for eighteen minutes, mounted with cold butter and aged parmesan, then shaved with black truffle at the table. Vegetarian by default and vegan on request.

Ingredients

  • Carnaroli rice, Piedmont
  • Wild chanterelle & king oyster mushrooms
  • Black winter truffle
  • 24-month Parmigiano Reggiano
  • Thyme butter, shallot, dry vermouth

Nutrition (per serving)
